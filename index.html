Good to know

A few quick answers before you begin.

Who are these courses for?

They are for NGO founders, fundraising teams, compliance officers and anyone who works with donors or Daankosh.

Do I need to take the levels in order?

Basic is the best place to start if you are new to Daankosh. Advanced and Masters build on the platform's wider workflows.

What happens when I choose a course?

You will continue to TrainerCentral, where you can open the course, lessons, quizzes, assignments and certificate.

Can I retry a quiz?

Yes. Quizzes allow retries, so you can review the lesson and try again.
